# Lectale logo concepts

**1. Ribbon Cut.** A bookmark-shaped cut in a Cobalt page turns the act of saving your place into the mark itself. The rounded page gives it the familiarity of a bookplate. Draw it as a single filled SVG path with an open cutout, and keep the V and the narrow strip to its left at least two pixels wide in the 24 px favicon.

**2. Margin Tab.** A Cobalt bookmark hangs over the left edge of an Ink page, echoing the ribbon that marks the current lesson in the contents list. Draw it as a rounded page path and a five-point ribbon. At 24 px, widen the exposed tab slightly and keep the V deep enough to read when both shapes share a single fill.

**3. Open Margin.** A Cobalt L frames the left and bottom edges of a Mist page. The small cut at the elbow recalls a bookmark, while the open field gives the page room to breathe. Use one filled path for each colour. At 24 px, drop the Mist fill and simplify the elbow to one broad triangular cut.

**4. Page Foot.** A broad, softly rounded L carries a short bookmark below its baseline. The silhouette has the quiet character of a publisher’s mark, with the tab suggesting a place to return to. Join the body and tab in one filled SVG path, using simple arcs for the curves. At 24 px, widen the tab and deepen its V so the bookmark remains visible.
