# EU marketplace, consumer, tax, and moderation readiness — v3.2

**Mandatory documented solo-operator self-review before enabling paid courses; no required outside-lawyer or compliance-department sign-off.** Payments + instructor-generated listings create legal roles beyond serverless code. Do not turn on paid checkout until merchant of record/supplier, true platform legal operator and complaint address, VAT treatment, tax invoices, dispute/refund responsibility, fee settlement and seller verification are documented. Instructors may be private individuals or traders; selling to EU consumers has additional obligations.

- EU-001 Terms of Service, instructor terms and paid-course terms: published before registration/onboarding; accurate seller identity, ownership/licenses, fees (platform takes 5% of agreed eligible net subtotal), VAT/gross price, payment fees, settlement timing, complaints, takedown, refunds, moderation and retention. Use versioned acceptance and immutable offer/purchase snapshots. Never invent a legal operator or jurisdiction for the platform.
- EU-002 Consumer checkout: show total price incl. applicable taxes, trader identity, main content/access characteristics, duration, technical requirements, statutory withdrawal details and clear payment-obligation button before payment. Digital-service vs digital-content classification affects 14-day withdrawal exception; do NOT automatically claim that immediate access eliminates withdrawal. Collect distinct express consent and acknowledgment only where legally valid and relevant; give purchase confirmation on a durable medium. Build automated refund/rights workflow backed by the operator's documented, jurisdiction-specific policy; do not falsely claim legal certification.
- EU-003 EU VAT/OSS: determine seller/merchant of record, place of supply and B2C VAT; invoice and reporting responsibilities. EU OSS may simplify qualifying B2C supplies, but do not assume the platform is never deemed supplier or that Connect solves tax obligations. Launch country restrictions until reviewed. Keep tax IDs and records only as necessary; leave sensitive KYC to Stripe onboarding where possible.
- EU-004 DSA applicability assessment (not a blanket assumption of all marketplace obligations): hosted course indexes and marketplace may be hosting/online platform/online marketplace services. Implement easy electronic notice-and-action for allegedly illegal content, complaint handling, reasons for restrictions, content/moderation logs and contact point. Where required, trader traceability/KYBC, seller information display, ads transparency and reporting obligations. Some DSA provisions exempt micro/small platforms; applicability must be documented rather than assumed. Protect minors; do not profile them for targeted ads.
- EU-005 Intellectual property and privacy: instructor warrants license to upload/share recorded lectures, PDFs, music, student images/audio and third-party materials; takedown process and counter-notice/appeals. Course replay links must not secretly expose private paid content.
- EU-006 Seller tax reporting (e.g. DAC7): assess whether paid-course marketplace activities are reportable (online courses are not automatically classified as personal services); retain advice and implement only obligations found applicable. Do not implement an unverified blanket DAC7 withholding rule.
- EU-007 Payment and mobile store rules: Stripe Connect connected-account verification and reconciliation; only webhook-confirmed entitlement. For Apple App Store/Google Play, disable external digital course purchases or links until target storefront policy, entitlement eligibility and fees are reviewed. Web-first paid sales default. Provide legal/policy pages in supported languages, versioned changes and accessible help.

### Paid course release gates

- LEGAL-GATE-01 Real legal operator, contact, processor contracts, cross-border data obligations reviewed. LEGAL-GATE-02 Consumer law and withdrawal policy reviewed, preview/paid scope clear. LEGAL-GATE-03 Tax merchant of record and net creator payouts calculated. LEGAL-GATE-04 DSA notice-and-action, contact/complaints and seller verification decisions documented. LEGAL-GATE-05 applicable EAA exceptions and WCAG/cookie/privacy readiness documented by owner. LEGAL-GATE-06 Native purchase policy vetted or native checkout explicitly disabled. LEGAL-GATE-07 Prices, terms, invoices/refunds/webhook identities tested.
