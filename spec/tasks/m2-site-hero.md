## Content, decided by the owner

- **Headline:** "Your course, online in minutes."
- **Subheading:** unchanged from the current hero: "Put your lessons, PDFs and notebooks in a GitHub repository, add one outline file, and share a link. Anyone can read it free, no account."
- **Primary button (gold):** "Open a course". It leads to where a visitor can add a course by link or browse the catalog: `/app/#/open` (the app's page with the link field and the catalog entry).
- **Secondary button:** "Make a course". It leads to the instructors section on the same page.

## Design

- **Right side:** a refined open book in the style of `hero-e.webp`: warm paper pages, a mulberry cover and the gold bookmark ribbon. Generate it as the shipped artwork with your image tool, on a transparent background, at 2x the display size.
  - Ship it as AVIF with a WebP fallback in a `<picture>`, with `width`, `height` and `alt=""`, since it is decorative.
  - The tabs from E may stay if they read cleanly; otherwise drop them.
- **Orbiting quotes:**
  - **The quotes:** 5 or 6 short ones from `docs/research/2026-11-22-learning-quotes.md`, rows 1 to 13 and 16, word for word, each with its author. Choose inspiring ones about learning and reading.
  - **Motion:** they move along an elliptical path around the book. The text stays upright, never on a curve, and never overlaps the book's centre or another quote. One lap takes 60 to 90 seconds, in CSS (`offset-path` or transforms).
  - **Pause control:** a visible "Pause quotes" toggle button with `aria-pressed`, a gold focus ring and a 44 px target, from one same-origin script. WCAG 2.2.2 requires it for motion lasting more than 5 seconds.
  - **Reduced motion:** with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the quotes sit still around the book, and the pause control is hidden.
  - **Screen readers:** the quotes are one static list of `blockquote` and `cite`, visually hidden but available. The moving copies are `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Composition:** fill the full 1440 width. The headline, subheading and buttons sit on the left, and the book with its orbit on the right. The product preview below may overlap the hero's lower edge.
- **Phones (390 px):** the headline, subheading and buttons come first. The book sits below at a smaller size, and the quotes become one quote line under it, still with no autoplay motion.
- **Dark mode:** the same composition. The artwork needs no recolouring, because the paper book sits on the dark page like a lit object.
- **Tokens:** every colour and size comes from `apps/site/src/site.css`.
